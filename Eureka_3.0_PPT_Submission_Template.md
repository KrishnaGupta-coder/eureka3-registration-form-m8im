## 📋 General Presentation Rules:
- **Total Slides:** Exactly **6 Slides**.
- **Header Top-Left Badge:** `<Your Team Name>` in a blue oval pill badge (Slides 2-6).
- **Header Top-Right:** `EUREKA 3.0!\nE-CELL ACEIT x AIC` (Slides 2-6).
- **Footer Bar:** Deep Navy Blue (`#0B4F9F`) with `@Eureka 3.0 Idea Submission Template` and Slide Number at bottom right.
- **Submission Format:** Submit as `.PPTX` or `.PDF` (File Name: `TeamName_Eureka3_Idea.pdf`).
