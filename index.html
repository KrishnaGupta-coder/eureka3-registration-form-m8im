About The Competition

What is Eureka 3.0!?

Eureka 3.0! (Road to Enterprise 2026) is Asia's Largest Startup Launchpad presented by E-CELL ACEIT & Arya Incubation Centre at Arya College of Engineering & I.T. in collaboration with E-Cell IIT Bombay. It empowers student innovators with access to 300+ Mentors, 45+ Incubators, ₹26L+ Equity-Free Grants, and 50+ Venture Capitalists.

Innovation & Ideation

Pitch your original business ideas, technological solutions, and creative prototypes to industry veterans and academic experts.

Incubation Acceleration

Get direct access to Arya Incubation Centre’s state-of-the-art lab infrastructure, coworking space, and legal/IP assistance.

1-on-1 Mentorship

Receive personalized advice and strategic guidance from successful founders, venture capitalists, and domain leaders.

National Recognition

Compete with top engineering and management teams nationwide for attractive cash awards, trophies, and investor exposure.
